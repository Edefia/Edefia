Dieu-Donne Edefia Ladzekpo is a technology entrepreneur, software engineer, and the founder of **Zine Solution**, a digital products and software venture headquartered in Accra, Ghana.

His work spans full-stack web and mobile application engineering, product architecture, and end-to-end software delivery. As a technical founder and engineering lead, he focuses on designing resilient web platforms, enterprise systems, and business automation tools that solve concrete real-world challenges.

Alongside software architecture and startup development, Dieu-Donne is committed to developer mentorship and technical education, actively collaborating with developer communities and academic tech initiatives to promote practical engineering skills, software craftsmanship, and technology entrepreneurship.

## Selected Projects

Key production platforms and software engineering systems demonstrating full-stack implementation, architectural design, and digital product execution:

### 1. ClassEase — Digital Venue Booking & Timetable Scheduling System
- **Description:** A smart campus resource management platform developed for the University of Cape Coast to streamline lecture hall reservations, examination scheduling, and automated clash-free timetable generation.
- **Tech Stack:** `Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `PostgreSQL` · `Prisma` · `Tailwind CSS`
- **Repository:** [`Edefia/ClassEase-new`](https://github.com/Edefia/ClassEase-new)
- **Live Deployment:** [class-ease-new.vercel.app](https://class-ease-new.vercel.app)

### 2. Metro Plus Kitchen — Kitchen Ordering & Operations Management Platform
- **Description:** A commercial restaurant ordering and management solution built for Metro Kitchen in Cape Coast, featuring an interactive digital menu, multi-channel order processing for delivery and pickup, and order fulfillment tracking.
- **Tech Stack:** `React` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `MongoDB` · `Tailwind CSS`
- **Live Deployment:** [metropluskitchen.com](https://metropluskitchen.com)

### 3. Zine Growth — Social Media Marketing (SMM) Services Platform
- **Description:** A marketing technology platform delivering automated social media services and client dashboards for agencies and digital marketers, featuring secure payment workflows and service lifecycle automation.
- **Tech Stack:** `React` · `Node.js` · `MongoDB` · `AWS` · `Stripe API`
- **Repository:** [`Edefia/zine-growth-new`](https://github.com/Edefia/zine-growth-new)
- **Live Deployment:** [zinegrowth.online](https://zinegrowth.online)

### 4. CareVo / CareBis Prime — Preventive Healthcare & Diagnostic Access Platform
- **Description:** A digital healthcare platform providing direct access to verified health screening services, medical consultations, partner diagnostic laboratories, and clear, accessible diagnostic reports.
- **Tech Stack:** `Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `PostgreSQL` · `Prisma` · `Tailwind CSS`
- **Repository:** [`Edefia/CareVo`](https://github.com/Edefia/CareVo)
- **Live Deployment:** [care-bis-prime.vercel.app](https://care-bis-prime.vercel.app)

### 5. ScholarOS (SchoolMS Pro) — Multi-Tenant Educational ERP System
- **Description:** An enterprise multi-tenant school operations platform featuring dynamic conflict-free timetable scheduling algorithms, time-locked period attendance management, installment fee calculators, and academic reporting across administrative and faculty roles.
- **Tech Stack:** `MongoDB` · `Express.js` · `React` · `Node.js` · `Tailwind CSS`
- **Repository:** [`Edefia/schoolMS-pro`](https://github.com/Edefia/schoolMS-pro)
- **Live Deployment:** [school-management-system-pro-ashy.vercel.app](https://school-management-system-pro-ashy.vercel.app)
